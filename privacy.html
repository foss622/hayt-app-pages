Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Hayt (shown on the Google sign-in screen as "Hayt Data Quality Mailer") is a private automation tool operated by one person for their own use. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

When the owner signs in with their Google account, Hayt can access the files in that account's Google Drive, and can send email from that account, as allowed by the permissions granted on the Google consent screen.

How data is used

Google data is used only to perform the tasks the owner requests: reading, creating, moving and renaming files in the owner's Drive, and sending the owner's own notification emails. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training general-purpose machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

Working copies of files and sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's own private server with access restricted to the owner. Google data is not sold, and is not shared with or transferred to third parties, except where required by law.

Limited use

Hayt's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is withdrawn, stored sign-in tokens stop working and are deleted from the server.

Contact
