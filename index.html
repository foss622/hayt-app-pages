Hayt

Hayt (shown on the Google sign-in screen as "Hayt Data Quality Mailer") is a private, single-user automation tool. It runs on its owner's own server and helps the owner organise their own files and send their own notification emails.

What it does

  • Files, renames and organises documents in the owner's own Google Drive (for example receipts and statements dropped into an intake folder).
  • Sends status and data-quality report emails from the owner's own Google account.

Who can use it

Hayt is not a public service. It is used only by its owner with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up.

How it uses Google data

Hayt accesses Google Drive only to read, create, move and rename files in the owner's own Drive at the owner's request. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. See the privacy policy.
